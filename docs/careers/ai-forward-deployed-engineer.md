<div class="role-hero">
  <div class="career-eyebrow">CAREER PROFILE <span>•</span> CUSTOMER-EMBEDDED AI ENGINEERING</div>
  <h1>AI Forward Deployed Engineer</h1>
  <p class="role-lede">Works directly with customers and business teams to design, build, integrate, and deploy AI systems that solve real operational problems.</p>
  <div class="career-intro-meta"><span>Applied AI</span><span>Software engineering</span><span>Customer collaboration</span></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">THE ROLE</div><h2>Bring AI from prototype into production</h2></div><p>Forward Deployed Engineers combine hands-on engineering with customer discovery, technical delivery, and practical problem-solving.</p></div>
<div class="role-overview">
  <div><span class="role-label">PRIMARY FOCUS</span><strong>Deploy AI solutions in real environments</strong><p>Build and adapt software, agents, integrations, and evaluation systems around a customer's actual workflows and technical constraints.</p></div>
  <div><span class="role-label">TYPICAL COLLABORATORS</span><strong>Customers, product, research, and engineering teams</strong><p>Translate business needs into technical plans and communicate trade-offs to both technical and non-technical partners.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">DAY-TO-DAY WORK</div><h2>What the work involves</h2></div><p>The balance between coding, deployment, and customer collaboration varies by employer and project.</p></div>
<div class="role-detail-grid">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Understand the problem</strong><p>Work with customer teams to map workflows, requirements, data sources, and success measures.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Build applied AI systems</strong><p>Develop applications, agents, APIs, retrieval systems, and integrations using suitable models and tools.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Deploy and evaluate</strong><p>Test reliability, performance, security, and usefulness before and after production rollout.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Improve the solution</strong><p>Debug real-world issues, gather feedback, and turn successful patterns into reusable tools.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">CAPABILITIES</div><h2>Skills that matter</h2></div><p>Current job descriptions emphasize strong software foundations, applied AI experience, cloud deployment, and communication.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Software and AI</strong><p>Python, TypeScript or JavaScript, APIs, LLM integration, RAG, agent workflows, and software testing.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Deployment and data</strong><p>Cloud platforms, SQL, data integrations, Docker, CI/CD, observability, and system debugging.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Customer delivery</strong><p>Requirements discovery, clear communication, technical judgment, collaboration, and responsible data handling.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">COLLEGE MAJORS</div><h2>Degrees that can lead to this role</h2></div><p>Recent employer postings identify several relevant technical degrees. A specific major is not universal, and equivalent practical experience may be accepted.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Most direct majors</strong><p>Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Strong related majors</strong><p>Information Technology, Information Systems, Machine Learning, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, and Electrical Engineering.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Specialized pathways</strong><p>Operations Research, Computational Science, Cybersecurity, or a domain degree such as Finance or Healthcare, paired with substantial software and AI training.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">BUILD YOUR PATHWAY</div><h2>Prepare for the work</h2></div><p>Many positions expect professional engineering experience, not only a degree or familiarity with AI tools.</p></div>
<div class="role-pathway">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Build engineering fundamentals</strong><p>Study programming, data structures, databases, APIs, mathematics, and software design.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Ship applied AI projects</strong><p>Build and evaluate an AI application that connects to real data and can be deployed reliably.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Practice technical communication</strong><p>Explain requirements, design choices, limitations, and results to people with different technical backgrounds.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-research-note"><div class="career-note-mark">i</div><div><strong>Research note</strong><p>Forward Deployed Engineer and AI Forward Engineer are different labels in this directory. Forward Deployed Engineer is a customer-embedded delivery role; AI Forward Engineer describes AI-native software development. Employer definitions can vary. Review current job descriptions for exact qualifications.</p></div></div>
