<div class="role-hero">
  <div class="career-eyebrow">CAREER PROFILE <span>•</span> STRATEGY &amp; ARCHITECTURE</div>
  <h1>AI Solutions Architect</h1>
  <p class="role-lede">Plans how AI systems fit into an organization's technology, data, and business processes — balancing value, reliability, security, and cost.</p>
  <div class="career-intro-meta"><span>System architecture</span><span>Cloud platforms</span><span>Responsible AI</span></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">THE ROLE</div><h2>Connect business needs with technology</h2></div><p>AI Solutions Architects shape the technical approach before and during implementation.</p></div>
<div class="role-overview">
  <div><span class="role-label">PRIMARY FOCUS</span><strong>Design practical AI solutions</strong><p>Translate organizational needs into secure, scalable designs that use suitable AI models and services.</p></div>
  <div><span class="role-label">TYPICAL COLLABORATORS</span><strong>Business leaders and technical teams</strong><p>Align stakeholders, developers, data specialists, and security teams around an implementation plan.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">DAY-TO-DAY WORK</div><h2>What the work involves</h2></div><p>The role combines technical design, communication, and an understanding of organizational priorities.</p></div>
<div class="role-detail-grid">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Understand requirements</strong><p>Identify business goals, constraints, users, and the problem an AI system should solve.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Design the architecture</strong><p>Select suitable models, platforms, data sources, and system components.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Plan integrations</strong><p>Map how AI services connect with existing applications, infrastructure, and workflows.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Manage trade-offs</strong><p>Address privacy, security, reliability, scalability, governance, and operating cost.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">CAPABILITIES</div><h2>Skills that matter</h2></div><p>Architects need enough technical depth to make sound design decisions and explain their implications.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Architecture foundation</strong><p>Cloud systems, software architecture, APIs, data platforms, and system design.</p></div>
  <div><strong>AI and governance</strong><p>Machine-learning fundamentals, model platforms, privacy, security, and responsible AI.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Strategic communication</strong><p>Requirements gathering, technical writing, stakeholder communication, and project planning.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">TOOLS &amp; TECHNOLOGY</div><h2>A typical toolkit</h2></div><p>Tool selection depends on the organization's existing infrastructure and the needs of the solution.</p></div>
<div class="role-tool-row"><span>Cloud architecture</span><span>Model platforms</span><span>Data services</span><span>API technologies</span><span>Security tools</span><span>Monitoring</span><span>Design documentation</span></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">GETTING STARTED</div><h2>Build your pathway</h2></div><p>Many architects develop experience in software, cloud, data, or systems engineering before specializing in AI.</p></div>
<div class="role-pathway">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Build technical experience</strong><p>Learn software, cloud infrastructure, data systems, and the fundamentals of AI.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Practice system design</strong><p>Design a solution around clear requirements, integration needs, and operational constraints.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Explain your decisions</strong><p>Present architecture choices, alternatives, risks, and the reasons behind your recommendations.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-research-note"><div class="career-note-mark">i</div><div><strong>Research note</strong><p>Employer expectations differ by industry and seniority. Check current job postings and relevant cloud or AI certification requirements before drawing conclusions.</p></div></div>
