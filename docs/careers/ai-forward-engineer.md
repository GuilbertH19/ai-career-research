<div class="role-hero">
  <div class="career-eyebrow">CAREER PROFILE <span>•</span> AI-NATIVE DEVELOPMENT</div>
  <h1>AI Forward Engineer</h1>
  <p class="role-lede">Applies AI-assisted development practices to prototype, build, and improve software experiences with speed and sound engineering judgment.</p>
  <div class="career-intro-meta"><span>AI-assisted coding</span><span>Rapid prototyping</span><span>Product development</span></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">THE ROLE</div><h2>Move from idea to working product</h2></div><p>This emerging title describes engineers who use AI deeply throughout the software development process.</p></div>
<div class="role-overview">
  <div><span class="role-label">PRIMARY FOCUS</span><strong>AI-native software development</strong><p>Use AI tools to accelerate implementation while maintaining responsibility for code quality and product outcomes.</p></div>
  <div><span class="role-label">TYPICAL COLLABORATORS</span><strong>Product, design, and engineering teams</strong><p>Work closely with users and teammates to turn needs into tested software experiences.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">DAY-TO-DAY WORK</div><h2>What the work involves</h2></div><p>The exact balance of coding, prototyping, and product work depends on the employer.</p></div>
<div class="role-detail-grid">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Prototype quickly</strong><p>Turn product ideas into working demonstrations and iterate based on feedback.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Use AI development tools</strong><p>Apply AI assistance to coding, debugging, documentation, and development tasks.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Review generated code</strong><p>Check correctness, security, maintainability, and performance before release.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Improve product workflows</strong><p>Connect AI capabilities to real user needs and refine the experience over time.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">CAPABILITIES</div><h2>Skills that matter</h2></div><p>AI tools can accelerate development, but strong engineering fundamentals remain essential.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Software engineering</strong><p>Programming, debugging, testing, APIs, system design, and code review.</p></div>
  <div><strong>AI-assisted development</strong><p>Evaluating AI-generated code, writing effective instructions, and choosing suitable tools.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Product judgment</strong><p>Rapid prototyping, user feedback, prioritization, communication, and practical decision-making.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">TOOLS &amp; TECHNOLOGY</div><h2>A typical toolkit</h2></div><p>Specific tools vary, and the title is not yet standardized across the industry.</p></div>
<div class="role-tool-row"><span>AI coding assistants</span><span>Git</span><span>Python</span><span>JavaScript</span><span>TypeScript</span><span>Testing tools</span><span>Prototyping platforms</span></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">GETTING STARTED</div><h2>Build your pathway</h2></div><p>Develop the ability to build software independently before relying on AI assistance.</p></div>
<div class="role-pathway">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Strengthen fundamentals</strong><p>Learn programming, data structures, debugging, version control, and software testing.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Build with AI assistance</strong><p>Use development tools on a small project and carefully review every generated change.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Show the process</strong><p>Document what you built, how you validated it, and where human judgment was important.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">COLLEGE MAJORS</div><h2>Degrees that can lead to this role</h2></div><p>This AI-native software role rewards strong product-building skills more than one specific degree title.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Closest technical majors</strong><p>Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, and Artificial Intelligence.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Product and design majors</strong><p>Human-Computer Interaction, Information Systems, Interaction Design, and User Experience Design, supported by substantial programming coursework.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Additional pathways</strong><p>Data Science, Applied Mathematics, or Digital Product Development can be useful when paired with software engineering fundamentals.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-research-note"><div class="career-note-mark">i</div><div><strong>Research note</strong><p>AI Forward Engineer is a relatively new and inconsistently defined title. Compare actual job descriptions instead of assuming every company uses the term in the same way.</p></div></div>
