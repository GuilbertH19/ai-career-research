<div class="role-hero">
  <div class="career-eyebrow">CAREER PROFILE <span>•</span> BUILD &amp; INTEGRATE</div>
  <h1>AI Engineer</h1>
  <p class="role-lede">Builds the software that turns artificial intelligence models into useful, reliable products and services.</p>
  <div class="career-intro-meta"><span>Software engineering</span><span>Machine learning</span><span>AI applications</span></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">THE ROLE</div><h2>From models to real-world products</h2></div><p>AI Engineers connect models, data, and software systems to solve practical problems.</p></div>
<div class="role-overview">
  <div><span class="role-label">PRIMARY FOCUS</span><strong>Build and integrate AI systems</strong><p>Develop applications that use machine learning or generative AI within products and business processes.</p></div>
  <div><span class="role-label">TYPICAL COLLABORATORS</span><strong>Product, software, and data teams</strong><p>Work across disciplines to translate requirements into dependable technical solutions.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">DAY-TO-DAY WORK</div><h2>What the work involves</h2></div><p>Responsibilities differ by employer, product, and level of experience.</p></div>
<div class="role-detail-grid">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Develop applications</strong><p>Build software features powered by machine learning or generative AI models.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Connect systems</strong><p>Integrate models with databases, APIs, and existing applications.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Evaluate performance</strong><p>Measure quality, reliability, speed, and operating cost.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Improve in production</strong><p>Monitor system behavior and refine solutions using testing and feedback.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">CAPABILITIES</div><h2>Skills that matter</h2></div><p>A strong foundation combines software development with an understanding of data and model behavior.</p></div>
<div class="role-skill-panels">
  <div><strong>Technical foundation</strong><p>Python, software engineering, data structures, APIs, and data pipelines.</p></div>
  <div><strong>AI knowledge</strong><p>Machine learning fundamentals, statistics, model evaluation, and generative AI.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Professional strengths</strong><p>Debugging, analytical thinking, communication, and collaborative problem-solving.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">TOOLS &amp; TECHNOLOGY</div><h2>A typical toolkit</h2></div><p>Specific technologies depend on the organization and the systems being developed.</p></div>
<div class="role-tool-row"><span>Python</span><span>Git</span><span>Cloud platforms</span><span>Model APIs</span><span>Jupyter</span><span>ML frameworks</span><span>Testing tools</span></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">GETTING STARTED</div><h2>Build your pathway</h2></div><p>Learning is strongest when technical foundations are paired with practical projects.</p></div>
<div class="role-pathway">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Learn the foundations</strong><p>Study programming, mathematics, data structures, and core machine-learning concepts.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Build practical projects</strong><p>Create applications that integrate an AI model and demonstrate how you tested its output.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Document your work</strong><p>Explain design choices, limitations, tools, and what you would improve next.</p></div>
</div>
