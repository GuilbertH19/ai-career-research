<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Government statistical agencies publish standardized information that can help compare occupations and labor-market conditions. Always check the geography, time period, occupational definition, and methodology.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH LANDSCAPE</div><h2>Recommended starting points</h2></div><p>Important concepts to investigate and compare.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">PRACTICAL EXAMPLE</div><h2>How to use this resource</h2></div><p>Connect the topic to a concrete research question.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel skill-project"><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH EXERCISE</div><p>For a career profile, identify the closest official occupation category, record the data year, compare national and regional figures where available, and explain when a job title does not map neatly to one category.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH METHOD</div><h2>How to investigate it</h2></div><p>Use a consistent method and keep track of evidence quality.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Government datasets are not always specific to emerging AI job titles. Explain the occupational proxy used and do not present a broad category as an exact salary for a new role.</p></div>
