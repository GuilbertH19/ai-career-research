# Research & Learning Resources

Use reliable sources and record the publication date for every statistic or major claim.

## Suggested source types

- Government labor statistics and occupational databases.
- University research and peer-reviewed publications.
- Reputable industry reports.
- Employer career pages and current job postings.
- Official documentation for AI and cloud technologies.

## Research checklist

- [ ] Record the author, title, publisher, and publication date.
- [ ] Save the original URL.
- [ ] Check whether a salary figure is national, regional, or employer-specific.
- [ ] Distinguish current data from predictions.
- [ ] Compare claims across more than one source.

## Citation reminder

Do not present salary estimates or job-growth forecasts without identifying the source, date, location, and methodology when available.
