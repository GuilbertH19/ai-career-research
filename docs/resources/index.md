<div class="career-section-heading compare-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">DOCUMENT YOUR SOURCES</div><h2>Research quality checklist</h2></div><p>Consistent source notes make your findings easier to verify, compare, and update.</p></div>
<div class="resource-checklist">
  <div><span>01</span><p><strong>Full citation</strong> — author, title, publisher, and publication date.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><p><strong>Original link</strong> — save the URL to the original source, not only a search result.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><p><strong>Geographic scope</strong> — identify whether the data is national, regional, or employer-specific.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><p><strong>Evidence type</strong> — distinguish measured results, expert opinion, and forecasts.</p></div>
  <div><span>05</span><p><strong>Limitations</strong> — record methodology, sample size, assumptions, and uncertainty when available.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="career-research-note"><div class="career-note-mark">i</div><div><strong>Citation reminder</strong><p>Do not present salary estimates or job-growth forecasts without identifying the source, date, location, and methodology when available.</p></div></div>
