<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>AI agents can be designed to interpret a goal, select from approved tools, and carry out a sequence of steps. The level of autonomy varies widely, and consequential actions require carefully designed permissions and review.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">PRACTICAL EXAMPLE</div><h2>A scenario to examine</h2></div><p>Connect the topic to a concrete research question.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel skill-project"><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH EXERCISE</div><p>A support workflow could classify an incoming request, retrieve relevant documentation, draft a response, and route it to a person for approval. Each stage needs clear limits and monitoring.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH METHOD</div><h2>How to investigate it</h2></div><p>Use a consistent method and keep track of evidence quality.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Evaluate task completion, tool selection, error recovery, permission boundaries, and the quality of human handoffs. Start with narrow tasks and explicit approval points.</p></div>
