<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>AI literacy, data skills, software engineering, critical evaluation, and communication can all be relevant to AI-enabled work. Demand differs by sector, location, seniority, and the actual tasks in a role.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">PRACTICAL EXAMPLE</div><h2>A scenario to examine</h2></div><p>Connect the topic to a concrete research question.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel skill-project"><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH EXERCISE</div><p>Compare several recent job postings for the same occupation. Record which AI skills are required, preferred, or absent, and note differences in experience level and industry.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH METHOD</div><h2>How to investigate it</h2></div><p>Use a consistent method and keep track of evidence quality.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Use dated job-posting samples and official labor statistics. Separate current hiring evidence from long-term forecasts, and avoid assuming a new tool automatically creates a new occupation.</p></div>
