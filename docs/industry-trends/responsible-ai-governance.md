<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>AI systems can raise concerns involving personal data, security, accuracy, bias, transparency, and accountability. Responsible adoption combines technical safeguards with clear organizational ownership and human oversight.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">KEY DIMENSIONS</div><h2>Governance dimensions</h2></div><p>Important concepts to investigate and compare.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">PRACTICAL EXAMPLE</div><h2>A scenario to examine</h2></div><p>Connect the topic to a concrete research question.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel skill-project"><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH EXERCISE</div><p>Before deploying an AI tool that handles customer information, map what data it receives, who can access it, how long it is retained, and what happens when the output is incorrect.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH METHOD</div><h2>How to investigate it</h2></div><p>Use a consistent method and keep track of evidence quality.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Identify the system's intended use, affected people, data flows, foreseeable failure modes, and review process. Consult applicable laws and recognized risk frameworks for the relevant location and sector.</p></div>
