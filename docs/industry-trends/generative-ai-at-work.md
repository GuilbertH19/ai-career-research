<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Generative AI can help people draft, summarize, search, classify, and explore ideas. Organizations need to assess where it improves a workflow, where it introduces errors, and what human review is appropriate.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">PRACTICAL EXAMPLE</div><h2>A scenario to examine</h2></div><p>Connect the topic to a concrete research question.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel skill-project"><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH EXERCISE</div><p>A company introduces an AI assistant for internal policy questions. A useful evaluation checks answer accuracy, source references, privacy, time saved, and how often employees need human support.</p></div>

<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">RESEARCH METHOD</div><h2>How to investigate it</h2></div><p>Use a consistent method and keep track of evidence quality.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>Measure outcomes rather than relying on demonstrations. Compare task quality and completion time before and after adoption, and document errors, costs, and user feedback.</p></div>
