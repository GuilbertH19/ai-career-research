<div class="career-section-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">OVERVIEW</div><h2>Why this matters</h2></div><p>Understand the context before drawing conclusions.</p></div>
<div class="skill-detail-panel"><p>The value and risk of an AI system depend on its setting. A model used to recommend products has different requirements from a system supporting clinical, financial, or public-sector decisions.</p></div>
