# Skills for AI Careers

AI careers combine technical knowledge with communication, critical thinking, and responsible problem-solving.

## Technical foundations

- **Programming:** Python and/or TypeScript, debugging, and version control.
- **Mathematics:** statistics, probability, and linear algebra fundamentals.
- **Data:** data preparation, databases, and data quality.
- **AI concepts:** machine learning, neural networks, generative AI, and model evaluation.
- **Software engineering:** APIs, testing, security, and system design.

## Professional skills

- Communication and teamwork.
- Critical thinking and problem-solving.
- Research and continuous learning.
- Explaining technical ideas to nontechnical audiences.
- Ethical judgment and awareness of limitations.

## How to build evidence

Create small projects, document your decisions, explain how you tested results, and keep examples of your work in a portfolio.
