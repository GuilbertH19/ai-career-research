<div class="career-section-heading compare-heading"><div><div class="career-eyebrow">SHOW WHAT YOU CAN DO</div><h2>Turn skills into evidence</h2></div><p>A portfolio is stronger when it explains the decisions and evaluation behind each project — not just the final result.</p></div>
<div class="career-criteria">
  <div><span>01</span><strong>Build</strong><p>Create a focused project that solves a real, clearly defined problem.</p></div>
  <div><span>02</span><strong>Explain</strong><p>Document your approach, tools, trade-offs, and design decisions.</p></div>
  <div><span>03</span><strong>Evaluate</strong><p>Show how you tested quality, reliability, and limitations.</p></div>
  <div><span>04</span><strong>Reflect</strong><p>Describe what you would improve and what you learned.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="career-research-note"><div class="career-note-mark">i</div><div><strong>Important context</strong><p>No single checklist applies to every AI job. Use current job postings to identify which capabilities matter for a specific role and experience level.</p></div></div>
